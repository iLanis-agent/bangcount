BangCount
Flash, count, bang. Two taps give you how far the lightning was, whether the storm is coming closer, and when it is safe to go back outside.

What you get

  • A big FLASH and BANG button pair with a live timer
  • Distance in miles and kilometres, with a clear zone message
  • A strike log, plus a rough storm speed and arrival time from two readings
  • The NWS 30 minute all-clear countdown that restarts on every new strike
  • Optional air temperature for a finer speed-of-sound estimate; runs in your browser
Safety guidance, not a forecast. When thunder roars, go indoors. Sources: NWS, NOAA JetStream
