How it works

Light arrives at once. Sound takes about 5 seconds per mile (3 seconds per kilometre). Seconds divided by 5 is miles, divided by 3 is kilometres. Thunder is only heard within about 10 miles, so no bang after 50 seconds means the strike was probably farther than that. If you hear thunder, go inside, and wait 30 minutes after the last lightning or thunder before going back out.

Optional air temperature for a finer estimate: (speed of sound = 331.3 + 0.606 × °C m/s, an approximation). The storm speed and arrival time are rough straight-line guesses from your last two strikes at least a minute apart.
